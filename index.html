HR & L&D Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much of the course fee is HRD Corp claimable?
Under HRD Corp's Allowable Cost Matrix (January 2026), course fees are claimable from your employer levy up to RM1,750 per employee per day for public programmes and RM10,500 per group per day for in-house programmes, subject to HRD Corp approval via e-TRiS and your company's available levy balance.
Who delivers the training?
Programmes are delivered by AeU-SPEED practitioner trainers — experienced AI specialists who build and deploy GenAI workflows and agents for organisations in Malaysia.
Online or on-site?
Public cohorts are delivered live online across Malaysia so companies with teams in multiple states can train together with zero travel, venue, or meal costs. Dedicated in-house runs can be delivered live online or on-site.
What are the class sizes?
Public live-online cohorts are capped at 20 participants so every employee receives direct coaching. Dedicated in-house cohorts (such as AI Leadership & Strategy) accommodate up to 18 employees per group.
Do participants receive a university certificate?
Yes. Every employee who completes the programme earns an Asia e University (AeU) micro-credential certificate that stacks toward SPEED professional certificates.
Can you run these programmes in-house for our company only?
Yes. Any of our 4 programmes can be run exclusively for your company with examples tailored to your industry and internal workflows. Select "In-house / custom" in the proposal form above.
