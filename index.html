HR & L&D Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much of the course fee is HRD Corp claimable?
Under HRD Corp's Allowable Cost Matrix (January 2026), course fees are claimable from your employer levy up to RM1,750 per employee per day for public programmes (RM1,000 for a half day) and RM10,500 per group per day for in-house programmes, subject to HRD Corp approval via e-TRiS and your company's available levy balance. Three of our four programmes are priced inside those caps, so for most employers the full fee is covered by levy you have already paid — see the claim line under each programme above.
Some providers advertise “100% HRD Corp claimable”. Is that accurate?
Treat that wording with care. HRD Corp does not pay a percentage of a fee — it reimburses from your own levy, up to the Allowable Cost Matrix caps, after approval. A programme priced at or below RM1,750 per employee per day can in practice be fully covered by your levy, which is what “100% claimable” usually means. Anything priced above the cap leaves a top-up for the employer. That is why we publish the cap, the fee and the claim line side by side, instead of a blanket 100% promise.
How does the e-TRiS claim actually work, step by step?
1) Tell us the programme and headcount — we prepare the quotation, course outline and trainer profile your application needs. 2) Your HR submits the grant application in e-TRiS before training starts and waits for HRD Corp approval. 3) Your team trains, with attendance recorded. 4) After training, submit the claim in e-TRiS with the attendance and completion records we supply — claims must be filed within 6 months. We prepare the documents; the application itself stays in your company's e-TRiS account, as HRD Corp requires.
How far in advance do we need to plan?
Plan 3–4 weeks ahead. Under HRD Corp's Employer Circular 2/2026 (effective 15 June 2026), the grant must be approved before training begins, and in-house training can only start at least 14 calendar days after grant approval. An approved grant cannot be modified — a change means a new application. Pick your month in the proposal form and we will work the e-TRiS timeline backwards from it with you.
What happens if our levy balance is not enough — or is about to be forfeited?
If your levy balance is below the fee, HRD Corp covers up to your balance (and the cap), and the shortfall is a normal top-up from your training budget — we show that split in the quotation, before you commit. Unutilised levy does not roll forward indefinitely and can be forfeited if left unused, which is why HR teams typically schedule claimable programmes before their levy anniversary rather than letting the balance sit idle.
Do we have to pay upfront, and who gets paid?
For approved HRD Corp Claimable Courses, the course fee is paid from your levy — employers commonly describe this as “no cash outlay” because the 1% monthly levy has already been contributed. Any amount above the cap or above your levy balance is invoiced to the company in the normal way. Fees shown exclude 8% SST where applicable. We confirm the exact split — levy vs top-up — in writing before any cohort is confirmed.
Which companies and employees are eligible?
The route is designed for employers registered with HRD Corp who are already contributing the monthly levy. It is the employer's levy, so places are booked by the company for its own workforce — this is corporate training, not individual enrolment. If you are unsure whether your company is registered or what your levy balance is, check in e-TRiS first, or note it in the proposal form and we will help you work out what to check.
What attendance is required for the claim to count?
Participants must attend at least 75% of the programme for the training to count toward the claim. We take attendance for every live-online session, flag absences to your HR contact during the cohort — not after it — and supply the attendance record with the completion documents for your claim.
Our staff are not technical and have never coded. Can they cope?
Yes — that is the audience these programmes are built for. GenAI at Work and the department tracks assume no coding at all: participants work in ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini and Claude, in the office tools they already use. Only Build Your First AI Agent is hands-on with automation tools (n8n and Copilot Studio), and even there the build is guided, step by step, with templates provided — no programming background is required.
Which AI tools and models will our team actually learn?
The stack your company is most likely already paying for: ChatGPT, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio, Google Gemini, and Claude — plus n8n and MCP (Model Context Protocol) for agent workflows, and Perplexity for research. Training is done on your team's real tasks, not demo data, and we teach staff how to choose between models — for example, when a smaller, faster model is the right call and when a frontier model is worth it — so the skill survives the next tool change.
Will participants leave with something working, or just slides?
Something working. GenAI cohorts leave with a department prompt library and standardised workflows for email, reports and meeting minutes. Agent cohorts build 4 working AI agents for real company workflows during the 2 days. Leadership cohorts leave with a 90-day rollout roadmap and governance rules. Every programme also ends with a company safe-use policy draft, so HR is not left to write one from scratch.
Can staff practise on our own company documents? Is that safe under PDPA?
Yes, with guardrails — and the guardrails are part of the curriculum. We teach the difference between consumer free accounts and enterprise accounts, what never goes into a public AI tool, how to anonymise staff and customer data, and how human review works before AI output is used. HR, candidate and customer data are covered explicitly in the HR track. The aim is to replace the shadow AI your staff may already be using at home with a governed, PDPA-safe way of working.
What are the class sizes — and is there a minimum for an in-house run?
Public live-online cohorts are capped at 20 participants, so trainers can coach individuals — the cap competitors publish is typically 18–20 for the same reason. Dedicated in-house cohorts run up to 18–20 per group depending on the programme; the Leadership cohort is priced per group of up to 18. In-house usually becomes better value than public seats once you are training around 8–10 people from one department — tell us your headcount and we will price both routes in the proposal.
Online or on-site? What about language and multi-state teams?
Public cohorts are delivered live online across Malaysia, so teams in KL, Penang, Johor and East Malaysia train together with zero travel, venue or meal cost — those costs are also not claimable the way fees are, so online delivery keeps the whole spend inside the claim. Dedicated in-house runs can be delivered live online or on-site at your premises. Sessions are delivered in English, with Bahasa Malaysia support for in-house cohorts on request.
What support do we get after the training ends?
Training that ends at the classroom door usually fades in a month. Build Your First AI Agent includes 3 months of post-training implementation support while your champions put their agents into production. All cohorts receive the full prompt libraries, templates and recordings/assets from their sessions, and HR receives a completion report by department — who attended, what was built, and where adoption support is still needed.
Do participants receive a certificate anyone recognises?
Yes. Every participant who completes a programme earns an Asia e University micro-credential certificate. Unlike a standalone attendance certificate, AeU micro-credentials are stackable — they count toward SPEED professional certificates, which gives staff a visible progression path and gives HR a retention story, not just a one-day course.
Can the content be tailored to our industry and our workflows?
Yes. Any programme can be run in-house with examples, documents and capstone tasks drawn from your industry and your departments' real workflows — candidate screening in HR, campaign work in Marketing, variance reporting in Finance, SOP handling in Operations. Tell us the department and the recurring task that eats the most time in the proposal form, and the cohort will rebuild that task during the training.
What do participants need on the day — laptops, licences, software?
A laptop and an internet connection. For Copilot-specific modules, participants get the most from the session if your company already licenses Microsoft 365 Copilot; for the other modules we work across ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, and trainers will flag clearly which features need a paid plan and which work on free tiers. No software installation or IT project is required before a public cohort — if an in-house run needs anything specific, we confirm it in the e-TRiS pack, before you apply.
Why a university, instead of a regular training provider?
Three reasons HR teams give us. First, the credential: a university micro-credential that stacks toward a qualification carries more weight with staff than a provider attendance cert. Second, delivery reach: AeU is built for online and blended learning for working adults, so nationwide cohorts are its normal mode, not an add-on. Third, continuity: SPEED can take a team from a 1-day GenAI cohort through agent building to a professional certificate on one pathway, instead of a new vendor search every quarter.
How do we measure whether this was worth it?
We build the before/after into the programme. Participants rebuild one recurring task in class and time both versions — email drafting, report preparation and meeting follow-up are the usual benchmarks, and published employer research consistently finds GenAI users reporting significant time savings (81% in the EY Work Reimagined survey of Malaysian employees). Your completion report gives HR the attendance, artefacts and time-saving examples to report back to management.
Can we reschedule, or swap participants, if plans change?
Participant names can usually be updated before the cohort starts — tell us as early as you can, because the e-TRiS grant is tied to the application. Date changes are tighter: because an approved HRD Corp grant cannot simply be modified, moving a confirmed in-house date may require a new application. Talk to us before you submit the grant if your dates are not yet firm, and we will sequence the application around that.
